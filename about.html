ABOUT SHARINGSPACE

A place for students
to share knowledge.

SharingSpace is a proposed collaborative publishing platform designed for students to share projects, technical knowledge, research and experiences with their community.

Purpose

Make it simple for students to discover useful ideas and publish their own work.

Security & trust

Proposed controls include secure authentication, authorisation, input validation, content moderation and privacy controls.

AI assistant

The prototype demonstrates an AI learning assistant using local response data. A future version could connect it to a real AI service through a protected backend.
